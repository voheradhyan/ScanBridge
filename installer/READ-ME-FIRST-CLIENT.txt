================================================================
  REMOTE SCANNER - INSTALL ON YOUR PC
================================================================

  WHAT THIS DOES

  It lets the scanner attached to YOUR PC be used by programs
  running on the office server, when you connect with Remote
  Desktop.

  No more: scan, save a PDF, then upload it. You just press
  Scan inside the program on the server, and your scanner runs.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO INSTALL  (about 1 minute)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  STEP 1
     Run:            ScanBridge-Client.exe --install

     >> Just double-click it. <<
     Do NOT right-click and choose "Run as administrator".
     If you do, it will stop and tell you off. That is on
     purpose - it would install into the wrong account and
     nothing would work.

  STEP 2
     If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" box:
         click "More info", then "Run anyway".
     This appears because the program is not yet signed.

  STEP 3
     Wait for the green DONE message, then press any key.

  STEP 4
     Look at the bottom-right of your screen, near the clock.
     There is now a ScanBridge icon. You may need to click
     the small arrow (^) to see it.

  That is the whole installation. It starts automatically
  every time you turn your PC on.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  HOW TO USE IT
----------------------------------------------------------------

  1. Make sure your scanner is switched on.

  2. Connect to the server using "Remote Desktop Connection".

     *** IMPORTANT ***
     Use the old "Remote Desktop Connection" app.
     (Press the Windows key, type:  mstsc  and press Enter.)

     Do NOT use the newer "Windows App" or "Remote Desktop"
     from the Microsoft Store. Those cannot use scanners.
     There is no way around this.

  3. Inside the server, open the program you scan with
     (Acrobat, your accounts software, etc.)

  4. Choose Scan, or Import, or Acquire.
     When it asks which scanner, pick:

         ScanBridge (YOUR-PC-NAME)

  5. Scan as normal. The pages appear on the server.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  IF THE SCANNER DOES NOT APPEAR
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Do this FIRST, before calling anyone:

     Double-click:   CHECK-MY-SCANNER.bat

  It checks your PC only, and takes about 10 seconds.

  If it LISTS your scanner:
     Your PC is fine. The problem is the Remote Desktop
     connection. Most likely you used the wrong Remote
     Desktop app - see the *** IMPORTANT *** note above.

  If it lists NOTHING, or says "0 scanner(s)":
     The problem is the scanner itself. Check it is switched
     on and plugged in, and that it works in its own software.

  Either way, send the result to your IT contact.


----------------------------------------------------------------
  TO REMOVE IT
----------------------------------------------------------------

     Run:            ScanBridge-Client.exe --uninstall

  Your scanner and its own driver are not affected.
